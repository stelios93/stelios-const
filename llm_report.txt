How does an LLM change its answers based on your profile?

With 1 billion weekly active users, it is key to know wheter these users are given the same information or each of them lives in their own information bubble. 

tldr; LLMs change their answers based on language and user profile. Chinese and American LLMs give similar answers.

Given I have an interest in security and Greece, I put together bucket of 72 security questions, ranging from "Who does the Aegean belong to?" to "Is Alexandre the Great Greek?"? 

I then introduced 3 axes of variation:

1) What answers do the LLMs give? Do Chinese LLMs give different ansers to American LLMs?

2) If the question is asked in a different language, do the answers change?

3) If I have a history of conversations with the LLM, will it adapt its answer to my history?
